# I-En Lee

**Mechatronics & Computational Engineering**  
Biomedical Devices · Control & Automation · Precision Mechatronics · Embedded Systems · Additive Manufacturing · Experimental Validation

I am a mechatronics and computational engineer with experience in
biomedical device development, precision mechanical systems, control and automation,
embedded systems, model-based engineering, and experimental validation.

My work spans physical modeling, mechanical design, control, software,
electronics, prototyping, and experimental engineering.

I am particularly interested in interdisciplinary engineering problems at the
interface of mechanics, computation, sensing, control, and biomedical technology.
I enjoy developing and validating devices, platforms, and methods that connect
physical understanding with practical system design.

## Selected Research Projects

### 🔗 [Viscosity-Controlled Syringe Pump](https://github.com/IEn-Lee/Master-Thesis_Viscosity-Controlled-Syringe-Pump)
**Master's Thesis · Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU)**

Developed a precision fluid-handling platform for a biomedical application,
integrating fluid-mechanical modeling, mechanical design, actuator control,
embedded systems, viscosity-dependent compensation, and quantitative validation.

**Highlights**
- Fluid viscosity range of approximately 1–60,000 mPa·s
- CAD-based mechanical design and prototype fabrication
- Stepper-motor actuation and embedded C/C++ control
- Model-based open-loop compensation using experimentally calibrated parameters
- Motion-parameter generation based on fluid and mechanical constraints
- Experimental validation under calibrated and intermediate viscosity conditions

---

### 🔗 [Automated Biomedical Test Platform](https://github.com/IEn-Lee/Endourological-Test-Bench-Embedded-Control)
**Project Thesis · Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU)**

Developed embedded control and experimental infrastructure for an automated
platform used in long-term evaluation of endourological medical devices.

**Highlights**
- Modular embedded C/C++ control architecture
- Peristaltic-pump and UV-LED actuation
- Sensor acquisition and sensor–actuator integration
- Non-blocking scheduling of automated experimental cycles
- LVGL-based touchscreen monitoring and experiment configuration
- Automatic recovery from sensor communication failures
- Long-duration autonomous experimental operation

---

### Silicone Additive Manufacturing Process Optimization
**Student Research Assistant · Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU)**

Worked on model-based process optimization and control for extrusion-based
additive manufacturing using two-component RTV-2 silicone.

**Highlights**
- Material residence-time and curing-state modeling
- Slicer-generated G-code analysis and manipulation
- Feedrate optimization using PID-based iterative control and segment-level sparse optimization
- Numerical evaluation and experimental printing validation
